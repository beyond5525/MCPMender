# Code of Conduct

MCPMender（协议修匠）is committed to a respectful, technically constructive,
and welcoming project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels, languages, and viewpoints.
- Discuss code and outcomes without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while still addressing risk
  directly.
- Protect other people's privacy. Remove tokens, private paths, logs, and
  configuration before sharing material.
- Respect licenses and credit the work of others.
- Accept maintainer decisions about security embargoes, release scope, and
  project safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or targeted insults.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, unsafe instructions, or misleading
  test results.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or refusal to stop after a maintainer request.
- Using public Issues to disclose an unpatched vulnerability after being
  directed to the private security process.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior threatens people, the project, or users.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel available on the
repository. When no private conduct channel is available, open an Issue asking
for private contact without including sensitive details. Security
vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers should handle reports as privately as practical, avoid conflicts of
interest, and apply consequences in proportion to impact and repeated behavior.
